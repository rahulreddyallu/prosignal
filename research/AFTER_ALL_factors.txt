READ THE SHAPE, NOT THE LEVELS
  gross/yr converts a rank IC to an annual return at a fixed
  0.10 -- a rule of thumb, not a measurement, and the argument
  `alpha_per_ic` exists so it can be replaced with a fitted
  number. What survives that assumption is the ORDERING: IC
  rises monotonically with horizon here, so a longer hold keeps
  more of whatever the edge turns out to be. What does not
  survive it is any absolute claim about profitability.
